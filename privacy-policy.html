Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Smart Games Suite Pro is an offline-first game. It does not require an account, does not send gameplay data to a server, and does not include third-party advertising or analytics code.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app may save player names, scores, settings, and local leaderboard entries in your browser or app storage so your game history can remain available on the same device.

Data Sharing

Smart Games Suite Pro does not sell, share, or transmit your personal data. Local game data stays on your device unless you manually export a session report.

Children

The app is designed as a general-audience game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting Data

You can clear local game data from the in-app leaderboard reset option or by clearing the app/browser storage on your device.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the Smart Games Suite Pro Google Play listing.
