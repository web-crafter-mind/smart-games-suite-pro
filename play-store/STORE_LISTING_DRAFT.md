# Smart Games Suite Pro - Play Store Listing Draft

## App Name

Smart Games Suite Pro

## Short Description

Play Tic-Tac-Toe, Word Guess, daily challenges, tournaments, profile progress, and smart AI offline.

## Full Description

Smart Games Suite Pro is an offline-first game suite built for quick play, local competition, and brain-training challenges.

Play Tic-Tac-Toe against another player or a stronger hard-mode AI, then switch to Word Guess for category-based puzzles, hints, timer mode, AI guessing, and two-player rounds. The Power Hub adds daily challenges, achievements, XP, streaks, and progress tracking, while the mobile-first profile and settings screens keep everything easy to manage offline.

Features:

- Tic-Tac-Toe with two-player, AI, timer, tournament, and style options
- Word Guess with categories, difficulty levels, hints, timers, and AI guess mode
- Daily hard word challenge
- Power Hub with XP, levels, streaks, and achievements
- Profile page with player level, XP, win rate, high score, streaks, and achievements
- Settings for theme, sound, music, haptics, and local data export/import
- Local leaderboards and session stats
- Offline-first play after installation
- Responsive layout for phones, tablets, Chromebooks, and desktop browsers
- No account required
- No ads or third-party analytics
